Do push-ups to unlock your social apps. Less scrolling, more health.

PushLife turns exercise into screen time. Pick the apps that steal your time (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube…)
and they only open while you have balance. To earn it, work out: every push-up is worth 1 minute.

• The camera counts your reps with on-device AI. No image ever leaves your phone.

• How-to guides for every exercise and posture tips while you train.
• Free time every day: half of your screen goal (1 h goal → 30 min); saved time stacks up to 2 hours.
• Workout programs: strength, challenges (0 to 10 push-ups, 100 push-ups) and stretches for any age, with levels that adapt to you.
• Emergency unlock: 5 minutes when you need it, paid back later with exercise.
• Optional adult-site blocking in every browser.
• Day streaks, levels, achievements, weekly challenges and progress charts.
• A daily goal that adapts to you and reminders at the time you choose.
• Free and no ads. Your account keeps your progress safe in the cloud. In English, Portuguese and Spanish.
